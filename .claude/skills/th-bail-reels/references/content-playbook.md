# T & H Bail Bond Agency: content playbook

## Who we're talking to
Mostly **family and friends of someone who was just arrested**: scared, confused,
often searching at night on their phone. Second audience: people who want to
understand bail *before* they need it. Tone: calm, warm, non-judgmental, fast and
clear. She's the steady voice on the other end of the phone. Never mock or shame
people who were arrested.

## Format differences
**Instagram Reels:** 15–45 s, slightly more polished, voice-led, carousel-like
tip slides do well, soft music bed under voice OK, caption can be longer.
Saves and shares matter most → "save this for later" angles.

**TikTok:** 7–25 s, hook in the first second, punchier text, faster scene changes
(1.5–3 s each), more myth/FAQ/"POV" framing, add a trending sound in-app.
Replies to comments as videos work well (pillar E).

## Captions & hashtags
Structure: **hook line** → 2–3 short lines of value → **CTA** ("Call 24/7 or tap
the link in bio") → 3–5 hashtags. Include the service area for local search (Wilmington, New Castle / Kent / Sussex County, Delaware).
Hashtag mix: 1–2 broad (#bailbonds #bail), 1–2 local (#delawarebailbonds
#wilmingtonde #newcastlecounty #kentcounty #sussexcounty), 1 topical (#knowyourrights #familysupport). No hashtag stuffing.
Best posting windows to test: 7–9 a.m., 12–1 p.m., 7–10 p.m. local; also late
night, when urgent searches happen. Track which wins after 2 weeks.

## Compliance (check every script)
T&H is licensed in **Delaware** (license #3000375457, shown on every video). Bail
agents in Delaware are licensed through the Delaware Department of Insurance; ask
Trisha to confirm any specific advertising rules she follows and note them here.
Safe defaults built into this skill:
- Show the **business name and license number** on every video (CTA slide + slide footers).
- **Never promise release, speed, or outcomes** ("we'll get them out in an hour", "guaranteed").
- **No legal advice.** Explain the bail process in general terms; say "talk to an attorney" for case questions.
- **Don't imply a connection to police, courts or jails.** No badges, official seals, or "official" wording.
- **No specific real cases**, names, mugshots, booking photos, or jail-roster screenshots.
- **Fees/premium:** don't state rates or discounts unless she confirms they're lawful and current in her state (many states set fixed premiums and ban rebates/discounts).
- **Paid promotion:** bail bond ads are restricted on some ad platforms (Google has prohibited them since 2018). Check each platform's ad policy before boosting a post; organic posting is the default plan.

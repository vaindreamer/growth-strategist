How the numbers work
  • Outlier = this post's views ÷ the creator's typical post (median). If the scan has fewer than 3 posts from a creator, it compares to the whole page. 5x means five times a normal post.
  • Velocity = views per hour since posting – catches things blowing up right now.
  • Engagement = (likes + comments + shares) ÷ views. On Reddit it's comments ÷ upvotes (how much discussion it sparks).
  • Viral score (0–100) blends all three. 🔥 70+ Viral · 🚀 45+ Outlier · 📈 25+ Above average.
  • Best pages to scan: a creator's Videos/Shorts/Reels tab, a search results page, a hashtag page, or a subreddit's Top listing.

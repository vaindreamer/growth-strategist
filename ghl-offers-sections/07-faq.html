FAQ

Questions before you book

Why is there a 3-month minimum for social media?

The first month is for learning what your audience responds to. Months two and three are where that turns into consistent results. Shorter than that, and you're paying for setup without seeing the payoff.

What do you need from me?

A kickoff call, access to your accounts and platform, your logo and brand colors, and photos or short clips of you and your business. After that, about 30 minutes a month to approve your content calendar.

I don't want to be on camera. Can we still do reels?

Yes. Reels can use clips of your space, your clients (with permission), text on screen, and voiceover. We'll plan formats you're comfortable with.

Can you build the landing page on my existing platform?

Yes. I build on GoHighLevel, ClickFunnels, Systeme.io and Kajabi. If you don't have one yet, I'll recommend the right fit for your offer and budget.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. You can move up a plan at any time, and adjust your plan after your first 3 months.

Do you run paid ads?

These packages cover organic content and landing pages. I also manage Google Ads and Meta ads, so ask about it on your call if you'd like ads added.

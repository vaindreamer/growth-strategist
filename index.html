FAQ

Common Questions About RPO

How is RPO different from a staffing agency?

A contingency agency sends resumes and moves on. RPO embeds a dedicated recruiting team into your process — full sourcing, screening, reporting, and ongoing optimization — as a true extension of your company.

How fast can we get started?

Most engagements are scoped and live within 1–2 weeks of kickoff, depending on the number and complexity of roles.

Do we still control final hiring decisions?

Always. We manage sourcing, screening, and process — your hiring managers make every final call.

How is pricing structured?

Pricing is scoped to your hiring volume and engagement model — Full, Project, or On-Demand RPO. Book a call and we'll walk through a custom quote.

Can this scale down if our hiring slows?

Yes — RPO capacity is built to flex with demand, so you're not locked into paying for volume you don't need.

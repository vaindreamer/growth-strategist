Do you guarantee sales?

No, and I'd be careful with anyone who does. Sales depend on your offer, price, market and how the system performs over time. What I guarantee is the build: your system goes live within 10 weeks of onboarding, or we keep working until it does.

Do I need to have an offer already?

Yes. This is for founders whose offer already has paying clients and real results. If you're still shaping what you sell, the audit call will tell you what to focus on first.

Is the content done for me?

Yes. We build the strategy, plan the month and create the posts, carousels and captions. You review and approve, and you stay the face and voice of your brand.

What's in the monthly fee?

Content execution, Meta ads management, funnel and email updates, GHL automation upkeep, a monthly strategy call with reporting, and Slack access between calls. Ad spend is paid directly to Meta and isn't included.

Why only 4 partnerships at a time?

Because I'm personally in your strategy every month. Keeping the number small is how every client gets the attention they're paying for.

What happens on the growth audit call?

It's a 45-minute call where we look at your offer, audience and current marketing, and find where buyers are slipping away. You'll leave with a clear recommendation, whether or not we work together.

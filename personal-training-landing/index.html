Questions

Frequently asked questions

What happens in the free consultation?

We talk through your goals, training history, schedule, and any obstacles that have gotten in the way before. From there we outline what a custom program could look like — no pressure, no commitment.

Do I need any experience to start?

No. Programs are built around your current fitness level, whether that's your first structured workout or your hundredth.

How often will my program change?

Your coach reviews and adjusts your plan on an ongoing basis as you progress, so the program never goes stale.

What if I need to pause or reschedule?

Life happens. Talk to your coach and sessions can be rescheduled based on availability.

How to use your kit

Set aside about 2 hours for your first session. A laptop is easiest, but everything works on a phone too.

  1. About YouFill in the form once. Unsure about something? Click "Fill with sample data" to see a finished example, then click "Start over" and type your own answers. Be honest. Only tick skills and tools you've really used.
  2. Make 3 practice projectsNo clients yet? This step matters most. Ideas: a Gmail folder system, a 1-week Canva social media calendar, a lead list in Google Sheets, a step-by-step guide (SOP). Save each one in Google Drive and set it to "Anyone with the link can view".
  3. ResumePick a style and click "Download PDF", then choose "Save as PDF".
  4. Portfolio websitePick a theme and click "Download website". Go to app.netlify.com/drop and drag the file in to get a free link. Paste that link back into Step 1 so it appears on your resume.
  5. Profile OptimizerChoose Upwork or OnlineJobs.ph and click "Load my drafts". Then work through "Fix these next" until your score is 85 or higher. Copy your title, overview and skills into your real profile.
  6. Apply every dayPaste each job post into the Proposal builder. Edit the draft so it sounds like you, and answer any questions the client asked.
Your answers save automatically in the browser you use. To switch devices or keep a safe copy, click "Back up my answers" and later "Restore backup". Nothing is uploaded anywhere, so your information stays private.

Your first 30 days

New VAs who get hired usually have two things: a specific, client-focused profile and consistent applications. This kit helps with the first. The plan below covers the second.

Week 1Finish About You, make your 3 practice projects, and download your resume and portfolio. Week 2Set up your profile and get your score to 85+. Complete identity verification and add your portfolio items. Send your first 5 proposals. Week 3Send 5–10 tailored proposals a day. Track them in a simple sheet: date, job, reply (yes/no). Week 4Look at your tracker. Few replies? Rewrite your first 2 lines and proposal opener. Getting replies but no hires? Offer a small paid trial task.

Do

  • Choose one kind of client to start with
  • Use their name and answer their questions
  • Keep proposals short: 120–180 words
  • Reply to messages quickly

Avoid

  • "Newbie, please give me a chance"
  • Copy-pasting the same proposal everywhere
  • Paying "agencies" that promise jobs
  • Moving payments off-platform early on

An honest note: no template or tool can guarantee you'll be hired. Results depend on your effort, your skills, the market and each client. This kit gives you a strong, professional starting point and a clear daily plan.

FAQ

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. Open the link and add it to your Home Screen. Downloading the PDF and website files is a little easier on a laptop.

Can I use it without internet?

Yes. Unzip the download and double-click index.html inside the kit folder. Your answers are saved separately for the online link and the offline file, so use "Back up my answers" to move them between the two.

I lost my answers!

Clearing your browser history or using private/incognito mode erases them. Make a backup after each session.

Do I need to pay for hosting my portfolio?

No. Netlify Drop and GitHub Pages are free for a simple one-page site.

FAQ

Questions

I have no VA experience. Will this still work for me?

Yes. Stage 1 shows you how your current job already maps to a VA role, and Stage 3 helps you build 3 sample projects so you have proof before your first client.

How much time do I need?

One to two hours a day after work. The 30-day action plan tells you exactly what to do each day.

Do I need to quit my job?

No, and please don't yet. Stage 8 gives you a readiness test and the numbers to check before you resign.

Is a client guaranteed?

No. Nobody honest can guarantee that. What the roadmap gives you is the exact process my 50+ students followed to land theirs.

How do I get the files?

Right after payment you get a download link for the PDF workbook and the Canva portfolio template, plus instructions to import it into Canva.
